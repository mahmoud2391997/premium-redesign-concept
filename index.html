Process

From audit to handover in six weeks

You see direction before any page is designed, so feedback lands early when it is cheap to act on. Timing depends on page count and is confirmed after the audit.

  1. Audit

    Review every current page, the content, the assets to retain and what users struggle with.

    Week 1

  2. Direction

    Two visual directions shown on your homepage. You choose one, or a blend.

    Week 1–2

  3. System

    Colour, type, spacing, grid and the core component library, built in Figma.

    Week 2–3

  4. Pages

    Every page at desktop, tablet and mobile, with interaction notes and prototype links.

    Week 3–5

  5. Handover

    A clean file, the written guide and a walkthrough call with your developer.

    Week 6
What you receive
  • Full redesign of every existing page in the new premium style
  • Desktop (1440), tablet (768) and mobile (375) layouts for each page
  • An organised Figma file with labelled components, variants and auto layout
  • A concise handover guide covering fonts, colours, spacing rules and UI elements
  • Clickable prototype with hover, focus and transition specs
